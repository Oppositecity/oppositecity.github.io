  • THREE INDEXES

  • CRYPSIS

    Hide any text or file inside a different, ordinary-looking text. Only someone holding the key can get it back.

    Loads clean at every size.

  • SATOR

    Rows read as columns, and the whole grid reads as its own half-turn. A census of the squares eleven languages hold.

    Could not start before: its census file was missing. Rebuilt from its own word lists.

  • PENTIMENTO, clearer on a phone

    The same piece. The ladder of layers has its own strip, the likeliest word is underlined and quoted in the top line, unlikely words are brighter, and the readout sits on black. Words no longer print over each other, and each new field opens turned to where its words are, not at empty space.

    A preview of a change to a live piece. Approve it and it replaces the current one.

  • EPHEMERIS, on the real sky

    The same chart, cast on this week's published orbits instead of assumed ones. Two of its bodies have burned up since it was made and are listed as gone; one has its orbit withheld and says so.

    A preview of a change to a live piece. Approve it and it replaces the current one.

  • NIMBUS, the plain and the particular

    The same cloud, drawn from two lists at once: the words the article says most, and the words most its own. Bold marks a word few other articles use.

    A preview of a change to a live piece. Approve it and it replaces the current one.

  • IDIOPHONE, the playable one

    The same instrument with a keyboard: tap to strike, hold to bow, slide to run along it. What you kept is there when you come back, and anyone's published objects can be opened by their handle.

    A preview of a change to a live piece. Approve it and it replaces the current one.
