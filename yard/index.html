Built and not published. None of these is on the hub, in the sitemap or in search. Each one waits on a yes or a no.

Ready to look at

  • THREE INDEXES

    A story read three ways at once: the motifs that survive retelling, the slot each occupies in the sequence, the whole tale it is a version of.

    Works on a phone and a laptop. Matching runs in the page.

  • FALL

    Sound in, sediment out. Nothing is filmed.

    Runs on its built-in test signal. Not yet tried with a real input.

  • CRYPSIS

    Hide any text or file inside a different, ordinary-looking text. Only someone holding the key can get it back.

    Loads clean at every size.

  • SATOR

    Rows read as columns, and the whole grid reads as its own half-turn. A census of the squares eleven languages hold.

    Could not start before: its census file was missing. Rebuilt from its own word lists.

  • PENTIMENTO, clearer on a phone

    The same piece. The ladder of layers has its own strip, the likeliest word is underlined and quoted in the top line, unlikely words are brighter, and the readout sits on black.

    A preview of a change to a live piece. Approve it and it replaces the current one.

  • IDIOPHONE, the playable one

    The same instrument with a keyboard: tap to strike, hold to bow, slide to run along it. What you kept is there when you come back.

    A preview of a change to a live piece. Approve it and it replaces the current one.

Working, needs a decision

  • RIG and CHIP

    The band's bench: find, cut and mangle a sound, and a chip synth with Nintendo pulse, triangle and noise and Sega FM.

    List on the band page, or keep as tools?

  • LODE

    A phone's magnetometer drawn as a field, from a phyphox recording or live.

    Needs a phone with phyphox to judge.

  • COLOR FIELD

    Flattens your camera to a single pixel. Point it at the world and get one colour back.

    Nothing shows until start is pressed and the camera is allowed.

  • VENN

    Every surface placed by the senses it works in.

    An experiment. It did not match what was pictured.
