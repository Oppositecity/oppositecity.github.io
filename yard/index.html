Ready to look at

  • IDIOPHONE, for a room as well

    A second way in: arm for a room, clap once, and the room is measured and goes under the keys. Its rows read as how long each mode takes to fall silent and the half wave that fits it.

    A preview of a change to a live piece. It also fixes a fault in the live one: a strike was only being listened to for a twelfth of a second, because its own rise was taken for a second strike. Tested with made-up recordings only, not a real room or a real phone. Approve it and it replaces the current one.

  • QUICK, on a typewriter ribbon

    Two colours, as on a ribbon: living words in white, newborns struck in bold, the dead left one generation in red. Set at typewriter pitch, so a word across reads as a word.

    A preview of a change to a live piece, from chat, 2026-11-21. Run headless at phone and laptop sizes. Approve it and it replaces the current one.

  • PALIMPSEST

    The opening sentence of a Wikipedia article, with every word that ever stood in it kept in its place. Drag through the years: white words stand, struck ones were scraped off, a gap is a word not yet written.

    New, 2026-11-16. Run headless on four subjects at phone and laptop sizes. On a phone a much-rewritten sentence runs a little past the screen and scrolls.

  • FALL

    Sound in, sediment out. Nothing is filmed.

    Runs on its built-in test signal. Not yet tried with a real input.

  • CRYPSIS

    Hide any text or file inside a different, ordinary-looking text. Only someone holding the key can get it back.

    Loads clean at every size.

  • SATOR

    Rows read as columns, and the whole grid reads as its own half-turn. A census of the squares eleven languages hold.

    Could not start before: its census file was missing. Rebuilt from its own word lists.

Working, needs a decision

    A phone's magnetometer drawn as a field, from a phyphox recording or live.

    Needs a phone with phyphox to judge.

  • COLOR FIELD

    Flattens your camera to a single pixel. Point it at the world and get one colour back.

    Nothing shows until start is pressed and the camera is allowed.
