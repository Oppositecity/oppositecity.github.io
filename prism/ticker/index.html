How it's priced. Each word in each post is looked up in human rating studies and scored 0–100 on that study's own scale. A ticker's level is the mean over every rated word posted in the last 60 seconds. “Everyday” is the same lens averaged over English weighted by how often words are used (SUBTLEX-US film and television subtitles), so the gap shows how Bluesky reads against ordinary speech. Change is measured from the level when you opened the page. Up is green, down is red; nothing else is coloured.

Driving it. The words that pushed the selected ticker furthest from everyday English in the last minute, weighted by how often they were posted. Small function words (is, the, have) count in the price but are left out of these lists. No posts, names or accounts are shown or kept; only word counts, in this browser, until you close it.

Sources. Posts: Bluesky's public Jetstream. Lenses: the ousiometer of Dodds and colleagues; NRC VAD Lexicon v2; labMT; Lancaster Sensorimotor Norms; Brysbaert concreteness; Glasgow Norms; Kuperman age of acquisition; the extended Moral Foundations Dictionary.
