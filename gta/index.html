GTA

Grand Theft Auto, 1997, the DOS version. It runs in an emulator on this phone. No ads, no trackers, nothing sent anywhere.

The page needs the game files once: a zip of the DOS game, such as the demo gta8demo.zip. Once loaded it stays saved on this phone, so you won't need it again.

Where to get it: search archive.org, or the download page of the site you were on.

Controls: ◀ ▶ turn · ▲ forward · ▼ back · FIRE shoots · CAR gets in or out of a car · BRAKE is handbrake, or jump on foot · ‹ GUN › change weapon · ESC is the menu, Y/N answer it. A keyboard works too. Turn the phone sideways.
