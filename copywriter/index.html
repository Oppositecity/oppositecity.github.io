THE DRAFT, WORD BY WORD

The numbers. Each lens is a study's own rating scale set to 0–100. On two-sided lenses (essence, pleasure, arousal, control) 50 is neutral; on the rest 0 is none. “In use” is everyday spoken English: every rated word weighted by how often it is said in film and television subtitles (SUBTLEX-US). A draft's figure averages its rated words, each time they occur. Words no study rated are shown in italic and are never guessed.

Sources and licences. Essence: the ousiometer of Dodds and colleagues, built on NRC VAD v1. Feeling: NRC VAD Lexicon v2 and labMT happiness. Body: Lancaster Sensorimotor Norms. Substance: Brysbaert concreteness and the Glasgow Norms. Life: Kuperman age of acquisition. Moral: the extended Moral Foundations Dictionary.
