A cellular automaton made of words, grown as a crossword.

standing, oldest first

A cellular automaton made of words. Each word is a cell, and its neighbours are the words that cross it. Two words are kin when they share at least two fields of meaning.

Every generation, all at once: a word crossed by the right number of kin lives. Too few, and it has nothing to mean against. Too many, and it drowns. Crossing a stranger kills it. An empty slot fills with the word closest in meaning to the kin it would cross. Two colours, as on a typewriter ribbon: words just born are struck in bold, and words that have just died stay one generation in red.
