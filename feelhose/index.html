How to read it. Each word in each post is looked up in human rating studies and scored 0–100 on that study's own scale. A lens's level is the mean over every rated word posted in the last 60 seconds. Gap is that level minus the same lens averaged over everyday spoken English (SUBTLEX-US film and television subtitles, each word weighted by how often it is said), so the zero line is ordinary speech. Move is the change since you opened the page. Colour marks the family; tap a line, a label or a tile to bring one lens forward, tap it again to let go.

Driving it. The words that pushed the lens in front furthest from everyday English in the last minute, weighted by how often they were posted. Small function words count in the levels but are left out of these lists. No posts, names or accounts are shown or kept; only word counts, in this browser, until you close it.

Sources. Posts: Bluesky's public Jetstream. Lenses: the ousiometer of Dodds and colleagues; NRC VAD Lexicon v2; labMT; Lancaster Sensorimotor Norms; Brysbaert concreteness; Glasgow Norms; Kuperman age of acquisition; the extended Moral Foundations Dictionary.
