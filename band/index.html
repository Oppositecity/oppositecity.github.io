Opposite City, the band: takes to take apart.

THE TAKES

A take is a recording the band posts on Bluesky. Each one keeps its own post, and every remix of it is posted as a reply underneath, so the take and everything made from it stay in one thread.

All of the takes are listed in XIMEREMIX, newest first.

THE REMIX PAGE

XIMEREMIX opens a take and plays it clean, exactly as it was recorded. The moment you touch it, it becomes an instrument, carrying on from the place where you stopped it.

OPEN XIMEREMIX

TAKE A TAKE APART

  1. Open XIMEREMIX and choose a take. Let it play, or touch it straight away.
  2. Hold the take to freeze it where your finger is, and drag to scrub through it. In steps mode it is cut at its attacks instead: tap one to play it, drag across to strum through them. Draw a loop to play one stretch over and over; tap twice to go back to the whole take.
  3. The settings change how it plays: speed, pitch, flip, tone, size, density, scatter, echo, room. The hold tab adds stretch, freeze, strings, shift, age and inside.
  4. Every change can be undone, a step at a time, and any setting can be put back to the take as it was.
  5. Bounce records exactly what you hear, if you want a file.

REPOST IT

  1. Every setting is written into the page's link as you go. The link is the remix: anyone who opens it hears the take the way you left it.
  2. Post sends the remix to Bluesky as a reply under the take's own post. Sign in with your Bluesky handle and an app password, never your real one; Bluesky makes app passwords in its settings.

BRING YOUR OWN

XIMEREMIX takes any sound, not only the band's: open a file in it, or drop one on it. In SHELLAC, set an in and an out while a 78 plays and clip it: the stretch goes to the clipper, where it can be trimmed, and from there to XIMEREMIX. Nothing you clip is posted unless you post it.
